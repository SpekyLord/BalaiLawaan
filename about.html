About Balai Lawaan

Balai Lawaan is a privately gated homestay and bed and breakfast in Iloilo City. Anj and her husband, Gregg Louie, opened their own home to guests and visitors who need a comfortable place to stay while in Iloilo.

Located in a quiet, safe, and friendly neighborhood, Balai Lawaan offers a peaceful setting while remaining accessible to transportation and other parts of the city. Its home-like atmosphere gives guests a welcoming place to rest and spend time.

The property has private guest accommodations and shared areas surrounded by greenery. Guests can enjoy spaces such as the courtyard, patio, dining area, and garden, along with amenities that support a comfortable stay.

Balai Lawaan is suitable for solo travelers, couples, small families, backpackers, tourists, and staycation guests. It provides a practical and relaxing place for visitors who want privacy, convenience, and a calm environment.

Through warm hospitality, Anj and Gregg Louie aim to help guests feel comfortable and at home during their visit. Balai Lawaan welcomes visitors looking for a peaceful homestay experience in Iloilo City.
